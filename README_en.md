> Welcome to the page for the mod "AnvilCraft: DearPlus"! This mod is an addon for *AnvilCraft*, themed around the power of **autumn**. Main content includes:

* **Autumnium Alloy**: A composite material crafted from copper ingots, terracotta and Autumnium components (birch leaves, cherry leaves, horn coral, etc.), the foundation of all Autumnium content
* **Autumnium Tools**:
  * **Autumnium Resonator**: Hold right-click to charge up, then shatter blocks with resonance
  * **Autumnium Ionocraft**: Grants creative flight when worn in the chestplate slot, consuming durability while flying
  * **Ringed Autumnium Broadsword** (3/5/7/9-Ringed): Upgradable through forging, featuring True Sweep and bonus damage against undead
* **Affix System**: Five affixes — Tranquil, Vying, Chopper, Decapitator, Dispossessor — granting unique abilities to tools
* **Reforging Panel**: An interface block for the Celestial Forging Anvil, with configurable filters to automatically reforge matching celestial bodies
