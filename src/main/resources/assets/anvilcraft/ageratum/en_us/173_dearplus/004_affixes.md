# Affixes

Autumnium tools bear a unique affix system, with five affixes in total.

## <color=#79422c>Tranquil</color> 偏安

- Cannot be enchanted through normal means (anvil, enchanting table, etc.)
- Cannot be disenchanted with a grindstone
- Disables all enchantments (including curses)
- Worn by the <ref item="anvilcraft_dearplus:autumnium_resonator"/>, <ref item="anvilcraft_dearplus:autumnium_ionocraft"/>, and the 3/5/7-Ringed broadswords

## <color=#fa9554>Vying</color> 逐鹿

Vying has a **level** (max 10); higher levels boost rare drops.

- Enchanting costs no experience and requires no levels
- Anvil enchanting costs 0
- Boosts the chance of **rare drops** (e.g. iron ingots and carrots from zombies): rare drop chance P becomes P + (1-P) × Vying level / 10 (guaranteed at level 10)
- Upgradable via Two-to-One Smithing or anvil merging: same levels add +1, different levels take the max (cap 10)
- Worn by the <ref item="anvilcraft_dearplus:ringed_autumnium_broadsword_9"/>

## <color=#d7af91>Chopper</color> 破竹

- Equal to **Sharpness** of the same level
- Not disabled by Tranquil

## <color=#b78766>Decapitator</color> 枭首

- Equal to **Beheading** of the same level
- Not disabled by Tranquil

## <color=#965f3b>Dispossessor</color> 豪夺

- Equal to **Looting** of the same level
- Not disabled by Tranquil
