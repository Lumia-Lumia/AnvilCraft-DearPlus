# <ref item="anvilcraft_dearplus:autumnium_ionocraft"/>

<color=#B78766> Soar on the Winds of Autumn </color>

<recipe id="anvilcraft_dearplus:autumnium_ionocraft"/>

An Autumnium glider that lets you soar the skies. Bearing the **Tranquil** affix.

## Properties

- Armor **+4**, durability **432**
- Grants **creative flight** when worn in the **chestplate** slot
- Consumes 1 durability per second while flying (like an elytra)
- Flight fails when durability drops to **1**, showing a broken texture; it never goes below 1
- Bearing Tranquil: cannot be enchanted or repaired in an anvil

## Repair

<recipe id="anvilcraft_dearplus:autumnium_ionocraft_repair"/>

- Cannot be repaired in an anvil; restore durability with an Autumnium Alloy Block in a crafting table
