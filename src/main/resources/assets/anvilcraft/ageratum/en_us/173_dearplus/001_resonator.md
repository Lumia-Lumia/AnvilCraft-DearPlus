# <ref item="anvilcraft_dearplus:autumnium_resonator"/>

<color=#B78766> The Resonance of Autumn, Piercing the Stars </color>

<recipe id="anvilcraft_dearplus:autumnium_resonator"/>

An Autumnium tool locked to its resonator form, bearing the **Tranquil** affix.

## Properties

- Durability **254**, mining level **Iron**
- Bearing Tranquil: cannot be enchanted, disenchanted, or repaired in an anvil
- Exists only in resonator form; cannot switch to specific tool modes

## Resonance Mining

**Hold right-click** on a block to charge up, then shatter the whole block with **resonance**:

- Charges for about 1 second, with an increasingly resonant hum
- Costs **128** durability per successful break (not reduced by Unbreaking)
- Cannot start when remaining durability is 128 or less
- Mining level is Iron: blocks above Iron tier (obsidian, ancient debris) drop nothing

## Repair

<recipe id="anvilcraft_dearplus:autumnium_resonator_repair"/>

- Cannot be repaired in an anvil; restore durability with an Autumnium Alloy Block in a crafting table
