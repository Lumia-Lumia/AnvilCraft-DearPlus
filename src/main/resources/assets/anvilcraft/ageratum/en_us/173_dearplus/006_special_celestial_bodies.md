# Hidden Celestial Bodies of the Forging Anvil

Besides ordinary stars and planets, the Celestial Forging Anvil conceals **special celestial bodies**. They will not appear on their own — you need to:

1. Place the correct **seed item** in the seed slot. Each celestial body corresponds to a set of candidate seeds, and the effective seed of this world is chosen pseudo-randomly from the world seed
2. Align the **anvil parameters** (Time/Space/Mass/Energy) with the target celestial body
3. Start searching — when both match, the hidden celestial body reveals itself

<color=#B78766> Good luck, explorer </color>

**Anvil Clue**: This planet's parameters should be **similar to the overworld** — if you have forged an overworld-like celestial body before, that same combination applies here.

## A Mole of Moles

<color=#666699> A tiny wonder in deep space </color>

**Anvil Clue**: This idea comes from a popular science book called *What If*, published in **2014**. One of its questions asks: what if we had a mole of moles? The book imagines hurling a mole of moles into **frigid** deep space, where they gather into a small planet. Each mole weighs about **100 g**, and a mole is mostly water, with a **density close to water**. Water is very hard to compress.

<ref item="minecraft:written_book"/>

**Overview**: Who on earth came up with that question?!
