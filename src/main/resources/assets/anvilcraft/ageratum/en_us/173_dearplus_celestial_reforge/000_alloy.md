# <ref item="anvilcraft_dearpluscelestialreforge:autumnium_alloy"/>

A composite material filled with autumn magic, the foundation of all Autumnium content.

<recipe id="anvilcraft_dearpluscelestialreforge:autumnium_alloy_from_components"/>

- Crafted shapelessly from <ref item="minecraft:copper_ingot"/>, <ref item="minecraft:terracotta"/> and one Autumnium component
- Available components (`is_autumnium_component`):
  - <ref item="minecraft:birch_leaves"/>
  - <ref item="minecraft:cherry_leaves"/>
  - <ref item="minecraft:horn_coral"/>
  - <ref item="minecraft:horn_coral_fan"/>
