# Ringed Autumnium Broadsword

A series of greatswords carrying the power of Autumnium, upgradable through forging. There are four tiers: **3-Ringed, 5-Ringed, 7-Ringed, 9-Ringed**.

## The Four Tiers

- **<ref item="anvilcraft_dearpluscelestialreforge:ringed_autumnium_broadsword_3"/>**: durability 480, attack 6, speed 1.6, bearing Tranquil
- **<ref item="anvilcraft_dearpluscelestialreforge:ringed_autumnium_broadsword_5"/>**: durability 540, attack 7, speed 1.8, bearing Tranquil
- **<ref item="anvilcraft_dearpluscelestialreforge:ringed_autumnium_broadsword_7"/>**: durability 800, attack 8, speed 1.8, bearing Tranquil
- **<ref item="anvilcraft_dearpluscelestialreforge:ringed_autumnium_broadsword_9"/>**: durability 1999, attack 12, speed 2.0, bearing Vying

## Forging Upgrades

- **3-Ringed**: crafted from Autumnium materials, the starting point of the series
- **5-Ringed**: 3-Ringed + 2 nuggets, processed by **Item Stamping**
- **7-Ringed**: 5-Ringed + 2 nuggets, processed by **Heating**
- **9-Ringed**: 7-Ringed + 2 nuggets, processed by **Two-to-One Smithing**

<recipe id="anvilcraft:two_to_one_smithing/ringed_autumnium_broadsword_9"/>

The **nugget** consumed during upgrades determines the affix gained:

- <ref item="minecraft:iron_nugget"/> (Iron) → **Chopper**
- <ref item="anvilcraft:copper_nugget"/> (Copper) → **Decapitator**
- <ref item="minecraft:gold_nugget"/> (Gold) → **Dispossessor**

## True Sweep

- Attacks trigger a wide-area sweep whether they **hit or miss**, dealing damage equal to the direct hit
- Sweep damage scales with the **Sweeping Edge** enchantment: `direct damage × √(level + 1)`

## Undead Slayer

- Deals **double** damage to **undead** mobs (zombies, skeletons, etc.)

## Repair

- Cannot be repaired in an anvil; restore durability with an Autumnium Alloy Block in a crafting table
