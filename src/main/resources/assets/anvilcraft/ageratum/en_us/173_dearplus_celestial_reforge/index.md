Hello! This is **YaanChio Lumia**.

*DearPlus: Celestial Reforge* is an addon for *AnvilCraft* themed around the power of **autumn**. All Autumnium items are crafted from <ref item="anvilcraft_dearpluscelestialreforge:autumnium_alloy"/> and come with unique abilities:

- **Autumnium Resonator** - a mining tool that shatters blocks with resonance
- **Autumnium Ionocraft** - worn equipment that grants creative flight
- **Ringed Autumnium Broadsword** - upgradable greatswords with True Sweep and undead slaying
- **Reforging Panel** - a Celestial Forging Anvil interface for automated filtering and reforging

Autumnium tools also feature a unique **affix** system: Tranquil, Vying, Chopper, Decapitator, Dispossessor.
